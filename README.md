TourGuideVanilla_TW

Complex of Modules fo TourGuide Vanilla adapted for Turtle-wow (https://turtle-wow.org/) private server
Included:

1. TourGuide Vanilla
2. TourGuide_TW_Attunements
3. TourGuide_TW_Class_Quests
4. TourGuide_TW_Dungeon_Quests
5. TourGuide_TW_Horde_1-60
6. TourGuide_TW_Professions
7. TourGuide_TW_Turtle_Quests

#TourGuide_TW_Attunements:
    -Attunements and Keys to Unlock Dungeons and Raids in vanilla and Turtle-wow added

#TourGuide_TW_Class_Quests
    -Class specific quests in vanilla and Turtle-wow added

#TourGuide_TW_Dungeon_Quests
    -Dungeon quests in vanilla and Turtle-wow added

#TourGuide_TW_Horde_1-60
    -Horde leveling guides in vanilla and Turtle-wow added

#TourGuide_TW_Professions
    -Professions guides in vanilla and Turtle-wow added

#TourGuide_TW_Turtle_Quests
    -Special quest-chains added in Turtle-wow
